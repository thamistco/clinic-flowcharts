All 43 review charts have verified UK patient information, with source name and 12 November 2026 content/link check date. There are 48 link placements across the charts, including national NHS pages and selected East Sussex support/service directories. Local eligibility must still be checked.

Source decisions and limits

  • Osteoporosis uses NG259 (29 July 2026), which replaced CG146. It does not repeat the obsolete assessment pathway.
  • Obesity separates NICE TA criteria, BNF product dosing and NHS England phased primary-care access. Semaglutide CVD prevention (TA1152) is a separate route and has a 2.4 mg weekly ceiling; the higher BNF obesity ceiling is not treated as a funding instruction.
  • Insomnia CKS June 2026, eczema CKS October 2026 and iron-deficiency anaemia CKS July 2026 were checked. Vaginal-discharge CKS remains dated February 2024; croup CKS May 2022. Those older dates are displayed, with limited scope.
  • Croup has one directly verified BNFC mild-croup oral dexamethasone card. No unverified paediatric maximum, liquid volume, severe-croup repeat protocol or asthma inhaler dose was added.
  • No new dose cards were inferred for eczema, osteoporosis, anaemia, vaginal discharge, dementia, bronchiolitis or bronchiectasis. Specialist treatments and full protocols remain in their original sources.
  • Older ESHT local croup, osteoporosis and child-asthma leaflets were inspected but not added as current advice. The croup leaflet advises steam, contrary to current NHS advice; the osteoporosis leaflet cites superseded CG146; the asthma leaflet predates later MART licensing. A local IV-iron leaflet also has an elapsed review date. The current national pages and verified local service directories are used instead.
