Review-copy progress

National condition charts checked one at a time · 11 November 2026.

21 of 21 national condition entries now have expandable review copies, including hypertension. Two are limited assessment/referral summaries because CKS remains dated 2022. Eight local MSK entries retain publisher originals rather than unverified new treatment summaries.

Source/layout checks are not clinical validation. No patient data is used. Failed checks and conflicts are not labelled unchanged guidance. Check live guidance and local policy at the point of care.

Scope and conflicts

  • NICE NG28: February 2026 initial medicine branches replace a metformin-only default. Renal function, frailty, ASCVD and other comorbidities change treatment.
  • NG59: psychological/combined-programme recommendations 1.2.13 and 1.2.14 were removed in July 2026; not repeated as current guidance.
  • Semaglutide: NICE ASCVD recommendation specifies Ozempic up to 1 mg weekly; BNF permits up to 2 mg weekly. Different scopes, not interchangeable prescribing instructions.
  • Heart failure: current text includes 2026 appraisal links despite the overview last-update date of September 2025. Overview dates alone do not prove currency.
  • Local thumb OA X-ray expectations and generic analgesia pathways differ from NICE NG226, which does not recommend routine OA imaging or routine paracetamol/weak opioids. Local originals are retained and not merged into a new national treatment rule.
  • Local spine guidance contains broader weak-opioid/analgesia advice than NG59 for chronic low back pain/sciatica. No combined medicine ladder is issued.
  • Local lumps referral wording is not a replacement for the current suspected-cancer pathway. No new sarcoma algorithm is issued from the local PDF alone.
  • Current BNF dose cards cover selected adult indications, not every drug. Inhaler device substitutions, DOAC dosing, insulin, HRT and paediatric dosing are deliberately not condensed without patient/product-specific checks. Individual Sussex Formulary drug entries have not been audited.
