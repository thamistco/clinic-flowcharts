Review-copy progress

Accuracy and UK-guidance audit · 12 November 2026

57 entries: 47 clinical review charts, two support pages and eight source-only local references. All 57 have blank EMIS prompts. The 49 review/support routes have 92 patient-link placements. This is a checked review copy, not NICE publication, clinical validation or a complete diagnostic/prescribing system.

What changed

Added adult fibromyalgia using the Royal College of Physicians diagnostic guideline and NICE NG193 chronic-primary-pain management. Corrected CKD referral thresholds, knee-dislocation scope, asthma MART licensing, migraine warning-feature scope, nasal-decongestant safety attribution and named-drug limits, anaemia wording, eligibility/source labels and source update dates. England Right to Choose now explicitly uses the national contract rule, not a narrower local FAQ. SEN/EHC links and all EMIS topic headings were reconciled.

What the audit establishes

Every authored chart's clinical statements were compared with its linked full UK recommendations, including indication-specific medicine headings where doses are supplied. Older evidence dates and source inconsistencies remain visible. Patient links were checked for destination, scope and relevant content; they are not a clinical endorsement of every sentence on an external website. No non-UK clinical guideline is used as an independent authority. Fibromyalgia criteria originate internationally but are explicitly adopted in the UK RCP guideline; use its full worksheet, not a score inferred from the summary.

NICE, CKS, BNF/BNFC, MHRA, NHS England, BTS, BSR, RCP and CoSRH have different roles. National clinical guidance is not replaced by a local access/commissioning rule. Technology-appraisal access, local referral availability and the final product licence still require a live patient-specific check. No medicine card is exhaustive. Individual Sussex Formulary entries were not audited.

Limits and details deliberately withheld

The CKS fibromyalgia investigations page could not be read; the test list instead comes from the full RCP guideline. CKS knee change history and selected CKS navigation pages were not consistently accessible; readable deep recommendations and visible revision dates were used, with no claim that a failed page was unchanged. Some NHS patient-page review dates are overdue; access dates are not revision dates. The insomnia source's currency page and scenario dates differ and are labelled. Unconfirmed drug-card extras were removed rather than guessed.

Eight local publisher PDFs are not rewritten, hosted or endorsed. Direct clinical quick-links are withheld after the high-risk review found conflicts and claims that could not be verified. The publisher index is kept as a reference with the correction notes below. A complete clinical validation of external PDFs or this site is not claimed. Use the current national source and local service advice before care.

Local publisher references: conflicts and limits

  • Foot & Ankle: East Sussex pathway

    Several timings and the consultant-agreement branch could not be reconciled to a current national source. A newer local Achilles/VACOped pathway complements this document. Do not treat the 2025 PDF as a validated all-condition algorithm.

  • Hand & Wrist: East Sussex pathway

    Mallet-finger and Dupuytren branches conflict with current reference sources: BSSH distinguishes closed injury (splint/radiograph and hand clinic within 72 hours) from open injury (same-day care). The Dupuytren radiotherapy citation is wrong; IPG368 requires special arrangements and notes limited evidence.

  • Hip: East Sussex pathway

    Local BMI/commissioning thresholds are not clinical exclusion rules: NG226 says do not exclude from referral solely for BMI, smoking, age, sex/gender or comorbidity. A newer 2026 hip/knee OA checklist is listed by the publisher. Some short timelines remain unverified.

  • Knee: East Sussex pathway

    Safety correction: suspected septic arthritis needs immediate specialist referral under NICE CKS, not bloods/antibiotics and watchful waiting. Do not use this PDF infection branch. Local BMI/commissioning rules are not NG226 clinical exclusion rules; a 2026 OA checklist is newer.

  • Shoulder & Elbow: East Sussex pathway

    Some fixed conservative-treatment timelines, including frozen-shoulder analgesia duration, could not be verified against current national sources. Use current condition-specific CKS/NICE recommendations and urgent referral exceptions, not this PDF as a complete clinical algorithm.

  • Spine: East Sussex pathway

    NG59 withdrew psychological/combined programme recommendations in July 2026; older wording in this PDF is not presented as current NICE advice. Weak opioids for acute low back pain are only considered if NSAID contraindicated, not tolerated or ineffective. Current emergency CES pathways take priority.

  • Lumps & Bumps: East Sussex pathway

    The adult sarcoma referral shortcut omits NG12 urgent ultrasound first for an unexplained growing lump, then suspected-cancer referral for suggestive/uncertain ultrasound with persistent concern. Children have distinct very-urgent pathways. This PDF is not a replacement for current NG12.

  • Physiotherapy: East Sussex pathway

    Routine service exclusions and symptom-duration rules must not delay urgent assessment or condition-specific referral. Availability and commissioner criteria are operational, not blanket clinical exclusions. Use the national red-flag pathway first.

Earlier release record: Decisions and checks for this expansion

  • Autism: CG142 adults, CG128 recognition/referral and CG170 under-19 support; older evidence dates remain visible. ADHD uses NG87, with specialist diagnosis/titration and agreed shared care, not a dose protocol.
  • Right to Choose uses current NHS England legal conditions and current local NHS information. No provider ranking, fixed waiting time, blanket approval-list restriction or guaranteed GP shared care is inferred. The local GP guide has provider/pathway caveats; confirm current contract, age and ongoing care. Private self-pay is separate.
  • Children's distressed-behaviour support considers unmet needs and other causes, with SENCo/EHC and age-banded family-hub/Amaze routes. Published courses do not imply current vacancies; some cohorts have already begun or ended.
  • Carer routes separate adult Care Act, parent-carer and young-carer assessments. Breaks/funding are not guaranteed. Provider grants are not personal family grants. Benefits use live eligibility, not frozen rates.
  • Joint pain compares sourced patterns/referral criteria; it is not an exhaustive diagnostic tree. RA tests must not delay referral, suspected septic arthritis needs immediate local-pathway referral, and NG65 uveitis is a same-day eye referral. No steroid trial or unverified medicine dose is added.
  • All EMIS prompts are blank. No unverified SNOMED/Read codes, pre-ticked normal findings or automatic negative red flags are included. Local testing, coding and approval remain required.

Earlier release record: Source decisions and limits

  • Osteoporosis uses NG259 (29 July 2026), which replaced CG146. It does not repeat the obsolete assessment pathway.
  • Obesity separates NICE TA criteria, BNF product dosing and NHS England phased primary-care access. Semaglutide CVD prevention (TA1152) is a separate route and has a 2.4 mg weekly ceiling; the higher BNF obesity ceiling is not treated as a funding instruction.
  • Insomnia CKS June 2026, eczema CKS October 2026 and iron-deficiency anaemia CKS July 2026 were checked. Vaginal-discharge CKS remains dated February 2024; croup CKS May 2022. Those older dates are displayed, with limited scope.
  • Croup has one directly verified BNFC mild-croup oral dexamethasone card. No unverified paediatric maximum, liquid volume, severe-croup repeat protocol or asthma inhaler dose was added.
  • No new dose cards were inferred for eczema, osteoporosis, anaemia, vaginal discharge, dementia, bronchiolitis or bronchiectasis. Specialist treatments and full protocols remain in their original sources.
  • Older ESHT local croup, osteoporosis and child-asthma leaflets were inspected but not added as current advice. The croup leaflet advises steam, contrary to current NHS advice; the osteoporosis leaflet cites superseded CG146; the asthma leaflet predates later MART licensing. A local IV-iron leaflet also has an elapsed review date. The current national pages and verified local service directories are used instead.
