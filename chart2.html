Atorvastatin: primary CVD prevention · BNF adult dose card

Adult, oral: 20 mg once daily; if needed increase at intervals of at least 4 weeks, maximum 80 mg once daily. Apply NICE risk/benefit criteria before starting.

Check interactions and muscle/liver risk; avoid in pregnancy, breastfeeding and active liver disease. CKD has a 20 mg starting branch; higher dose needs renal-specialist agreement if eGFR <30. BNF flags prevention doses/indications as differing from product licences; this is not a generic hyperlipidaemia starting dose.

Sumatriptan: acute migraine, oral · BNF adult dose card

Selected prescription regimen for adults aged 18-65: 50-100 mg once; repeat 50-100 mg after at least 2 hours ONLY if migraine recurs after response. No second dose for the same attack if the first dose did not work. Maximum 300 mg in 24 hours.

Not the pharmacy-sale regimen (which has a 100 mg daily maximum). Contraindicated with IHD/coronary vasospasm, prior MI/stroke/TIA, peripheral vascular disease, mild uncontrolled or moderate/severe hypertension. Check interactions, renal/hepatic status and seizure risk; avoid severe hepatic impairment. Stop and assess chest/throat tightness. Elderly use is unlicensed; pregnancy/breastfeeding outside this card.
