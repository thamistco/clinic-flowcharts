Adult oral acute sore throat: 500 mg four times a day OR 1,000 mg twice daily for 5-10 days.

Penicillin hypersensitivity and full interaction/organ-function checks apply. NICE notes 5 days may achieve symptomatic cure; 10 days may improve microbiological cure. Alternatives/allergy/pregnancy need the full live source.

Colchicine · BNF adult dose card

Adult oral acute gout: 500 micrograms 2-4 times daily until symptoms relieved; maximum total course 6 mg. Do not repeat within 3 days.

Narrow therapeutic window: overdose can be fatal. Check renal/hepatic impairment and CYP3A4/P-glycoprotein interactions before use; some combinations require avoidance. Older adults may need adjustment. This is a flare regimen, not ULT prophylaxis.
