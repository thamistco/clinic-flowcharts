Tirzepatide: eligibility is not access

NICE: BMI ≥35 plus ≥1 weight-related comorbidity, with ethnic adjustment and commercial terms. England primary-care rollout is narrower: currently cohorts I and II, ≥4 qualifying comorbidities with BMI ≥35 (adjusted where applicable). Confirm local service and wraparound support.

Tirzepatide · adult weight management · BNF adult indication-specific dose card

Subcutaneous: 2.5 mg once weekly for 4 weeks, then 5 mg weekly for ≥4 weeks. If needed, increase by 2.5 mg at intervals ≥4 weeks, maximum 15 mg weekly. Assess benefit if <5% loss after 6 months at highest tolerated dose. BNF licensed BMI criteria are broader than NICE funding criteria.

Avoid in pregnancy; stop ≥1 month before planned pregnancy. Oral contraception: add barrier or switch to non-oral for the first 4 weeks and 4 weeks after every dose increase. Pancreatitis warning, dehydration, gallbladder disease, slowed gastric emptying and aspiration risk. Review other medicines, especially insulin/sulfonylureas. Renal/hepatic impairment: KwikPen excipient caution. Use current product instructions; do not combine incretin medicines.

Semaglutide: two distinct NICE routes

TA875 weight management: specialist multidisciplinary service, maximum 2 years, ≥1 comorbidity and BMI ≥35, or BMI 30–34.9 with specialist-referral criteria; adjust ethnicity thresholds. TA1152 is a separate CVD-prevention route with BMI ≥27 and defined established CVD.

Semaglutide (Wegovy) · adult weight management / separate CVD indication · BNF adult indication-specific dose card

Subcutaneous: 0.25 mg weekly ≥4 weeks, then 0.5 mg ≥4 weeks, 1 mg ≥4 weeks, 1.7 mg ≥4 weeks, then usual maintenance 2.4 mg weekly, as tolerated. BNF BMI ≥30 weight-management branch permits an increase to 7.2 mg weekly after ≥4 weeks on the previous dose. BMI 27–29 weight-management and CVD-risk branches maintain 2.4 mg weekly. This higher BNF ceiling is not a NICE funding instruction: TA1152 CVD prevention specifies no more than 2.4 mg weekly; check the applicable local commissioned route.

Avoid pregnancy and breastfeeding; effective contraception during treatment and for ≥2 months after stopping; stop ≥2 months before planned pregnancy. Avoid end-stage renal disease; hepatic caution. Pancreatitis/dehydration/gallbladder and anaesthesia warnings apply. Prescribe the correct brand and indication; Ozempic/Rybelsus diabetes regimens are not this regimen. Consider stopping for <5% weight loss after 6 months. Read live monograph for new visual symptoms and other safety updates.

Live indication-specific source · accessed 12 November 2026. No separate monograph revision date displayed.

Liraglutide or orlistat: different criteria

Liraglutide TA664: BMI ≥35 (32.5 in specified ethnic groups), non-diabetic hyperglycaemia AND high CVD risk; secondary-care specialist tier 3 service. Orlistat: BMI ≥30, or ≥28 with risk factors. Do not treat these as interchangeable entry criteria.

Liraglutide · adult weight management · BNF adult indication-specific dose card

Subcutaneous: start 0.6 mg once daily, increase by 0.6 mg at ≥1-week intervals to 3 mg once daily. Stop if <5% initial weight lost after 12 weeks at maximum dose. The diabetes dose is different. NICE TA664 has additional specialist-service and eligibility restrictions.

Avoid pregnancy; severe hepatic impairment and CrCl <30 mL/min for weight management. Mild/moderate hepatic caution. Review pancreatitis, dehydration, gallbladder disease and aspiration risk. Breastfeeding requires individual assessment using the live monograph. Use an appropriately licensed weight-management preparation, not a diabetes-only product.

Live indication-specific source · accessed 12 November 2026. No separate monograph revision date displayed.

Orlistat · adult weight management · BNF adult indication-specific dose card

120 mg up to 3 times daily, immediately before, during or within 1 hour of each main meal. Omit if the meal is missed or contains no fat. Continue beyond 12 weeks only if >5% initial weight has been lost (BNF wording). Follow the full NICE/product review criteria.

Contraindicated in chronic malabsorption and cholestasis. Use the live interactions source and check fat-soluble vitamin needs. BNF pregnancy advice is use with caution; breastfeeding: avoid (no information). Weight management in pregnancy needs a separate clinical assessment. Reduced-fat diet helps gastrointestinal effects. Do not infer an OTC 60 mg regimen from this prescription card.

Live indication-specific source · accessed 12 November 2026. No separate monograph revision date displayed.

Daridorexant · adult chronic insomnia meeting TA922 · BNF adult indication-specific dose card

50 mg once nightly, within 30 minutes before bedtime. 25 mg may be appropriate in some patients. Use 25 mg with moderate hepatic impairment or relevant moderate CYP3A4 inhibitors as directed by the live monograph; avoid severe hepatic impairment ; check the current interactions source before use.

Contraindicated in narcolepsy. Review CNS depressants, alcohol, respiratory disease, mood/suicide risk, pregnancy and breastfeeding. Leave about 9 hours between dosing and driving/machinery. Do not take a second dose in the same night. Review within 3 months and discontinue for inadequate response.
