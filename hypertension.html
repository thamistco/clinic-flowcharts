What is included, and what is not?

Adult hypertension, including routine diagnostic checks, thresholds, step 1-3 class recommendations and routine targets. Four BNF drug examples. The pilot stops before step-4 prescribing and excludes CKD, type 1 diabetes, pregnancy-related care and secondary/condition-specific pathways. Selecting a button does not certify applicability.

Source discrepancy: NICE's update history records an October 2026 CKD clarification to 1.4.26. Its current recommendations page labels 1.4.26 as once-daily drugs and places a CKD signpost just before it. CKD prescribing is therefore excluded here; no amendment has been inferred. The headline last-updated date is 26 February 2026.
