EMIS Web

A template should prompt, not assume

These are build specifications, not importable or clinically approved templates. No patient information belongs on this website.

  1. Check access with your practice administrator. Open Configuration > Template Manager. Menu names may vary.
  2. Create a folder and a new template. Keep it in Draft while testing.
  3. Add coded entries only where the clinician actively selects the finding. Use text pick lists with free text for detail. Never pre-tick normal findings, negative red flags or counselling.
  4. Add blank prompts for the agenda, history, examination, assessment, uncertainty, plan and safety-netting.
  5. Make follow-up ownership, route and timing explicit. Add a diary entry if appropriate.
  6. Test on a practice-approved test patient. Ask your trainer or template lead to check coding and saved output before activation. Avoid automatic triggers until locally tested.

This copies headings only. It does not insert clinical findings, prescriptions or patient details.

Condition-specific build ideas
  • Sore throat: symptom duration, FeverPAIN components, examination actually done, treatment decision and safety-netting. Link NG84 rather than hard-code antibiotic rules.
  • UTI: symptoms, pregnancy possibility, systemic features, risk factors, testing and follow-up. Keep prescribing in the live local policy.
  • Chronic review: symptoms and control, adherence and adverse effects, observations, monitoring due and agreed follow-up.
  • MSK: site and duration, trauma, function, relevant red flags actively assessed, examination, working diagnosis, plan and return advice.

Clinic rhythm

Try one change, then review it

  • For one clinic, record where time goes: history, examination, decisions, notes or admin. Review the pattern with your trainer.
  • Bring out the whole agenda early: "What else did you want to cover?" Agree priorities and arrange follow-up for what cannot safely fit.
  • Use concise notes and blank prompts. Do not shorten safety checks or pre-fill normal findings to meet a timer.
  • Agree the plan, safety-netting and follow-up before the patient leaves. Close the record while details are fresh.
  • Discuss appointment length, catch-up time and protected admin with your trainer.
